Getting Started in the Ocean

Living in Hawaiʻi, the ocean is part of everyday life — whether you're bodyboarding, fishing, or swimming. Before entering the water, it’s important to understand safety and respect local conditions.

Step 1: Read the Ocean Before You Go In

Always observe ocean conditions before entering the water. Even calm-looking water can have strong currents.

  • Check surf reports before going out
  • Watch for rip currents and strong waves
  • Ask locals if you're unsure
  • Never force unsafe conditions

Step 2: Start with Beginner-Friendly Activities

Begin with simple, low-risk activities to build confidence in the water.

  • Bodyboarding: Learn wave timing near shore
  • Fishing: Relaxed shoreline activity
  • Shallow snorkeling/diving: Explore safely

Step 3: Use Proper Gear

Basic equipment improves safety and comfort in ocean conditions.

  • Rash guard or UV protection shirt
  • Reef-safe sunscreen
  • Proper equipment for your activity
  • Floatation aid if you are learning

Local Ocean Advice

Respect the ocean at all times. Conditions can change quickly, so awareness is essential.

“When in doubt, don’t go out.”
