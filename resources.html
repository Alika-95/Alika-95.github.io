Helpful Ocean Resources

This page includes useful tools, videos, and websites to help you learn ocean safety, improve your skills, and stay informed about local conditions in Hawaiʻi.

  • National Weather Service Hawaii — Local weather and ocean conditions.
  • Hawaii Beach Safety — Information on currents, surf warnings, and beach safety.
  • Surfline — Surf reports and ocean conditions.

Learning & Beginner Guides

  • SurferToday — Beginner bodyboarding techniques.
  • Take Me Fishing — Beginner fishing guides.
  • PADI — Diving and underwater exploration.

Local Tip

In Hawaiʻi, ocean conditions can change quickly. Even if the water looks calm, always check surf reports and ask locals if you're unsure about an area. Respecting the ocean keeps you and others safe.
