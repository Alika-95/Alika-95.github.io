Helpful Ocean Resources

This page includes useful tools, videos, and websites to help you learn ocean safety, improve your skills, and stay informed about local conditions in Hawaiʻi.

Ocean Safety & Conditions

  • https://www.weather.gov/hfo — Check local weather and ocean conditions in Hawaiʻi before heading out.
  • https://hawaiibeachsafety.com — Information about currents, surf warnings, and beach safety tips.
  • https://www.surfline.com/ — Surf reports and ocean condition updates.

Learning & Beginner Guides

  • https://www.surfertoday.com — Beginner guide to bodyboarding techniques.
  • https://www.takemefishing.org — Simple guide for starting shore fishing.
  • https://www.padi.com — Intro to diving and underwater exploration.

Local Tip

In Hawaiʻi, ocean conditions can change quickly. Even if the water looks calm, always check surf reports and ask locals if you're unsure about an area. Respecting the ocean keeps you and others safe.
